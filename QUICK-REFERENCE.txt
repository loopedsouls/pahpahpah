┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛

  [1] 🟢 BITMASK
      Effect:     Shoots 5 pies in a cone
      Cooldown:   3 seconds
      Use:        Clear crowds, maximize hits
      
  [2] 🔵 ALPHA MASK
      Effect:     Dash forward + 0.5s invincibility
      Cooldown:   4 seconds
      Use:        Escape danger, dodge through enemies
      
  [3] 🟠 DATA MASK
      Effect:     Confuse ALL enemies for 5 seconds
      Cooldown:   6 seconds
      Use:        Crowd control, buy time
      
  [4] ⚪ SURGICAL MASK (Passive)
      Effect:     Heal +1 HP every 5 hits
      Cooldown:   None (always active)
      Use:        Sustain, aggressive playstyle
      
  [5] 🟣 SHADER MASK
      Effect:     Slow-motion for 3 seconds
      Cooldown:   5 seconds
      Use:        Precision aiming, dodge bullets

  • Keep moving to avoid enemy shots
  • Build combos for higher scores and audience
  • Use Bitmask (1) on crowds for maximum hits
  • Save Alpha Mask (2) for emergencies
  • Data Mask (3) is great when overwhelmed
  • Equip Surgical Mask (4) early for sustain
  • Use Shader Mask (5) against Shooters

  • Collect masks every 3 kills
  • Don't let audience hit 0%!
